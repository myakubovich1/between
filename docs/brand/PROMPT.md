# Between: Open Interval identity

Created October 29, 2026 using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and one finishing edit. This is a new mark, rather than the previous lowercase-b recolor. Two opposing book pages frame an open interval; the Pear bookmark identifies a useful place to focus between classes. The existing native wordmark and supplied application palette remain.

The final generated master is `Between-Open-Interval.png` (1254 × 1254, opaque). Production exports are an opaque 1024 × 1024 iOS icon and a 384 × 384 in-app BrandMark. Exporting sizes with macOS sips does not change the artwork. Generated artwork can have small tonal variations; SwiftUI color tokens retain the exact palette values.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished iOS app icon and in-app brand mark for Between, an independent University of Florida library and study-space finder that helps students make useful study time between classes. Create ONE original, professionally resolved geometric symbol, not a concept sheet. Design idea: 'the open interval' — two bold opposing book-page/bookend forms frame a deliberate clean vertical gap, like a welcoming study nook or doorway within an open book. A single small Pear bookmark shape at the top of the middle gap gives a focus point; it must belong to the geometry, not float as decoration. The book/space reading should be immediate, with a distinctive minimal silhouette and generous breathing room. Calm, contemporary, friendly and confident; soft precise corners, balanced visual mass, vector-like flat design, excellent legibility at 32px. Colors: opaque full-bleed solid Cornflower #6AA6DA square background, solid Canvas #FBFBF8 main forms, one restrained Pear #E1E5AC bookmark accent. Use crisp clean edges and absolutely flat color fills, not gradients. Center symbol at approximately 62–68 percent of canvas width, optically centered vertically. Output a single square high resolution image, ideally 1024x1024. No lettering, no wordmark, no text, no outlines, no tiny page lines, no clock hands, no map pin, no stacked unrelated symbols, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, no mockups, no border, no baked-in rounded-square corner mask; iOS supplies the icon mask. Do not use any UF, alligator, university seal, or competitor logo. This must be a fresh brand identity rather than a recolor of an existing logo.

## Finishing edit prompt

Refine this exact logo for production. Preserve the existing two opposed book-page forms, central notched bookmark, scale, spacing, proportions and alignment exactly. Change only the finish: remove every trace of texture, grain, lighting, shading, gradient, vignette or paper effect. The entire square background must be one perfectly uniform Cornflower #6AA6DA fill. Both page forms must be one uniform Canvas #FBFBF8 fill. The central bookmark must be one uniform Pear #E1E5AC fill. Crisp, flat, vector-like solid color boundaries with clean antialiasing. This is a real app logo, not a rendering. No shadows, outlines, text, corner mask or extra objects. Opaque square. Output high resolution, ideally 1024x1024.

The previous logo assets and their prompt are retained in `archive/`; the original green master remains in `Between-logo.png`. No UF or competitor mark was referenced. Name/trademark clearance and App Store distribution remain separate from this artwork implementation.

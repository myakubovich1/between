# Between Study Spaces: TestFlight beta privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy describes the current guest-only TestFlight beta of Between Study Spaces, an independent UF library discovery app. Between is not affiliated with the University of Florida.

## Data on your iPhone

Between stores saved libraries, study preferences, a manually chosen starting building, an optional next building and study deadline, and a cache of public library information on your device. These preferences are not synchronized to a developer-operated account. You can clear local saved places and preferences from You → Clear saved places & preferences.

## Optional location and campus searches

You can browse and plan using a manually selected building without granting location access. If you choose Use my location, iOS supplies a foreground location sample for walking-route planning. The app does not record a location trail or request background location access. Foreground samples are held in memory and cleared when active use ends. Apple MapKit processes route destinations and any chosen origin under Apple's privacy practices.

Campus-building search sends your building search terms to UF's public campus directory and may use Apple MapKit as a fallback. Those providers receive the request and normal network metadata, such as an IP address. A manually selected building and next-stop plan can remain in local preferences until cleared.

## Public UF data and bookings

The app retrieves building/area headcounts, opening schedules and room availability from UF Libraries, LibCal and the public SafeSpace display infrastructure used by UF. Requests include the relevant library and, for room searches, selected date, time, duration, category and room size. These services receive normal network/request metadata. Building counts do not identify individual students and do not guarantee available seats.

When you continue to a booking, Between opens UF's official LibCal site in your browser. UF handles any sign-in, eligibility checks, reservation information, confirmation and cancellation under its own policies. Between does not receive your UF password, browser authentication cookies or reservation confirmation. Exact-time room links are held in a short-lived in-memory result and excluded from the app's saved JSON model. Your browser handles its own history and website data.

## Accounts, analytics and sharing

This beta does not enable Between accounts or community contributions, and its build contains no configured community-backend URL or key. It does not upload personal study preferences or a location trail to a developer-operated backend. No advertising or custom analytics service is configured. Public-data providers and Apple process their own service data under their privacy practices.

If you participate through TestFlight, Apple processes beta installation, diagnostic and feedback information through TestFlight/App Store Connect. Feedback you choose to submit, including screenshots and comments, can be made available to the developer through Apple's testing service.

## Retention and choices

Local preferences and cached public data remain on the device until cleared, replaced or removed with app data. Room availability becomes stale quickly and is refreshed while active. Provider-side retention of network requests, browser sessions, bookings and Apple beta feedback is controlled by the relevant provider; this app does not promise deletion from those providers.

You can decline location, select a manual starting building, clear local saved data, stop using TestFlight or remove the app. A UF booking is managed using UF's confirmation email; removing Between does not cancel a UF reservation.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this beta or its data handling, open an issue in the [Between project](https://github.com/myakubovich1/between/issues). This public issue tracker should be used for general questions and reproducible app bugs; avoid posting private account or reservation details. Changes that introduce Between accounts, contributions, analytics or new data collection will require an updated policy before those features are enabled.

Provider information: [Apple privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [UF privacy](https://privacy.ufl.edu/privacy-policies-and-procedures/onlineinternet-privacy-statement/), [UF study spaces](https://uflib.ufl.edu/using-the-libraries/studyareas/).

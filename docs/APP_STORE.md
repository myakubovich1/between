## Description

A little time between commitments can be enough to get something done. Between helps you compare study spaces around the University of Florida and understand how much of your gap is actually usable.

Set your time, choose a manual origin or use your location, and optionally add your next destination. Between considers walking, a buffer and known opening information when planning your study time. Browse library details, designated quiet floors, access caveats and recent student seat and sound observations. Save your useful places for another day.

Contribute a quick update when you're there. Conditions are clearly labeled with recent-report counts and observation age. No recent reports means unknown, not an empty building. Community observations are not reservations or guaranteed seat counts. Official room-reservation links open the university's existing service.

Guest browsing and local saves need no account. Apple sign-in is used for contributions, and location is optional. Between is an independent capstone project, not affiliated with UF. Availability of each feature depends on the configured live release and current curated data.

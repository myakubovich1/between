# Privacy, terms and support draft

These are owner-review drafts, not published policies. Supply an operator name and real public contact route, choose an effective date and confirm deployed providers/retention before publication. No placeholder contact address is shipped in the app.

## Privacy draft

Between helps you choose a place to study. You can browse, set a manual origin and save places without an account. Saved places and preferences stay on your device.

If you sign in with Apple, our authentication provider stores the account identity needed to recognize your session and any email Apple supplies. Authentication credentials on your device are kept in Keychain. We do not publish your identity or a history of where you study.

When you contribute, we store the selected place or floor, structured seat and optional sound conditions, server acceptance time, an account reference, a proximity category and moderation status. Other people receive summaries and opaque observation references. Only recent reports influence conditions; reports expire from the aggregate after 30 minutes and are purged from private storage after 24 hours at the next hourly cleanup. Flags are removed with their report; mute settings stay private until your account is deleted.

Using location is optional. We use a foreground sample for the immediate route or, if you choose, a limited nearby-report check. The report endpoint discards raw coordinates after that check and stores only the resulting proximity category. No continuous tracking or location trail is collected. Apple Maps also processes route requests under its policies. We do not use advertising identifiers, sell study activity or run advertising analytics.

You can clear local favorites/preferences from You, sign out, flag inaccurate observations or mute a contributor from an evidence panel. You can initiate account deletion in You. Confirming with Apple lets us revoke the authorization and delete your account and associated reports, flags and blocks. If a service fails, we show the failure and provide a retry path. A limited moderation operation audit without account identity is retained for 90 days. The final policy must state the owner's actual operational log and backup retention, processors, region, contact route and any rights required in the jurisdictions where the app is offered.

## Terms draft

Between is an independent study-space discovery project and is not affiliated with the University of Florida. Published venue policies, opening schedules and entry rules are controlled by the venues and can change. Follow campus and venue rules and check official hours/access before traveling.

Community seat and sound conditions are short-lived observations. They are not seat reservations, numerical occupancy measurements, accessible-route certifications or guarantees that adjacent seats are available. A nearby-report signal does not establish an exact floor, identity or truthfulness. Directions use address destinations and may not identify a usable or accessible entrance.

Contribute only current conditions you have personally observed. Do not submit deliberately false reports, spam, use the service to target another person, evade rate limits or interfere with the service. Reports may be removed and contribution access suspended for misuse. Use the evidence panel to flag concerns or mute a contributor, or contact the operator through the published support route. The final terms must include the actual operator and applicable dispute/consumer provisions selected by the owner.

Room-reservation links open the university's existing service. Between does not issue booking confirmations or control inventory. There are no subscriptions, ads or public text/photo posts in this release.

## Support page draft

- How to browse without location: use Find → origin → choose a campus library.
- How to understand data: open a place → Where this comes from. Gray/missing data means unknown; it does not mean empty.
- How to report a problem: open the evidence panel and flag the observation. Mute hides that contributor's influence from your signed-in results without revealing their identity.
- How to remove your data: You → Delete account; use Clear saved places & preferences for local data.
- If a report fails: your choices remain. Recheck the conditions and confirm before retrying. Do not repeatedly submit old observations as fresh.
- If deletion fails: your account remains disabled for new contributions until deletion succeeds. Reauthenticate with the same Apple identity and retry, or use the owner's real support contact.

**Owner input required:** a monitored contact route, response expectations, operator name and public HTTPS support URL. Add them before release; do not publish this owner-input note as a functioning support contact.
